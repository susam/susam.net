Nerd Quiz #3 is the third release of Nerd Quiz. It is a single-page HTML application that lets you test your nerd level with a short quiz. Each question in this quiz is handcrafted from everyday moments of reading, writing, thinking, learning and exploring.

This update adds five new questions about topics such as computing history, graph theory and Unix. Visit Nerd Quiz to try the quiz.

A community discussion page is available here. You are very welcome to share your score or discuss the questions there.
